Data Protection & Privacy

Data protection compliance is not an obligation, but the foundation of corporate trust.

In the digital economy, data has become one of the most valuable assets; as its value grows, so do the legal obligations and regulatory expectations around its protection.

Our Data Protection Expertise

Since the early days of the Turkish data protection law (KVKK), we have advised numerous domestic and international companies on compliance projects, and we also support companies operating under the GDPR.

KVKK & GDPR Compliance Projects

We carry out KVKK and GDPR compliance work along with data inventory and data flow analyses.

Registry, Consent & Disclosure

We support data registry processes and the preparation of explicit consent and disclosure notices.

International Data Transfers

We advise on international data transfers and data processing agreements.

Breach & Crisis Management

We stand by our clients in data breach management, crisis counsel, and processes before the data protection authority.

Our Approach

Every organization has a different operating structure, data processing activities, and operational needs; we therefore assess each project according to the organization's specific risks.

We develop solutions that are practical, sustainable, and aligned with business processes.

We see data protection not merely as a matter of legal compliance, but as a core element of corporate reputation and sustainable growth.
